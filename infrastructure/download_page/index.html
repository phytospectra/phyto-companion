Phytospectra Desktop

Point the app at the folder that holds your NMR or LC-MS data. The analysis runs on the Phytospectra cloud; your report — DOCX plus a zipped publication bundle — is written back into the folder you choose.

How it works

  1. Sign in — the app opens your account page in the browser; sign in there and the session is handed back to the app.
  2. Choose the data folder — Bruker experiment directories, zipped Bruker exports, and mzML / mzXML / mgf files are detected automatically.
  3. Run the analysis — processing happens on Google Cloud; only peak lists and metadata leave your workstation.
  4. Get the report — report.docx and a zipped report package appear in your chosen output folder.
  5. Buy tokens — purchases are handled on the account page (Stripe checkout); the desktop app never sees card data.

Requirements

macOS 12+ (Apple Silicon or Intel): unzip and drag Phytospectra.app to Applications; on first launch, right-click the app and choose Open to bypass Gatekeeper for the unsigned build. · Windows 10/11 (64-bit) · Internet connection · A free account includes a monthly analysis allowance; heavier workloads use tokens.
