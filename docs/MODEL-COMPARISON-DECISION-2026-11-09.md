# Model comparison and fine-tuning decision — 2026-11-09

**Decision: fine-tuning NOT JUSTIFIED.** The pre-registered gate requires repeated interpretation errors despite the correct candidate and decisive evidence being in the payload. No qualifying inference or expert-adjudicated evidence is available. This is a decision under missing evidence, not proof that fine-tuning could never help. Do not train, download models, upload data, or incur provider charges on this basis.

## Evidence and limitations

- `benchmark/blinded_pilot/PROTOCOL.md` describes four curated NMR-only smoke fixtures, not independent experimental cases. Their public keys and source structures mean these are public regression tests, **not a blinded benchmark**; scientific benchmark denominator is zero. The worksheet reviewers are null; proposed decisive correlations and C004 abstention remain unadjudicated. No paired MS/NMR arm exists yet.
- `benchmark/fine_tune_gate/FINETUNE-DECISION-GATE.md` states the gate and requires rights-cleared expert labels and leakage-resistant partitions. The existing notebook is a template, not evidence of a run.
- The offline harness under `benchmark/model_comparison/` locks prompts, packet/input hashes, identities, settings and repeat slots and reports failures, citations, costs and usage. It cannot establish semantic support or pretraining non-contamination. Imported provider metadata is self-reported.
- No model inference or comparison was performed for this decision. `ollama`, `llama-cli`, and `llama-server` were not on PATH. The installed `lms` inventory command failed because its LM Studio daemon/installation was unavailable; therefore local model availability could not be established. This does not prove no model exists elsewhere. No accuracy, cost-effectiveness or tuned-versus-untuned claim is made.

## Prerequisites to reconsider

1. Independently expert-review and adjudicate candidate truth, decisive observations, sufficient/ambiguous/abstention status and evidence-support rubric; assemble the protocol's 20–30 independent, reviewed cases, with documented sample linkage for NMR-only, MS-only and paired arms where applicable. The current four public fixtures remain regression only.
2. Freeze a leakage-resistant evaluation: separate development, untouched test and where possible source-held-out test, grouping standardized canonical structures, scaffold/compound families, salts, stereoisomers and replicates. Confirm train/dev/test and held-out source exclusions; inspect known contamination and rights per record. Keep private evaluator keys and review materials away from inference; public keys cannot provide blinding.
3. Pre-register locked prompts, model/provider immutable versions, seed/repeat plan, output limits, candidate sizes, tolerance, metrics, costs and failure handling. Run baseline arms on identical eligible payloads with explicit authorization and data/provider rights; no external inference for private or uncertain-rights data without permission. Report denominators, missingness, repeated attempts, citations and expert-adjudicated support separately.
4. Demonstrate a **repeated, expert-reviewed interpretation failure** on development cases where the correct candidate and decisive evidence are demonstrably present. If retrieval misses the structure, fix retrieval; if the peak is absent, fix extraction; if labels/filenames leak, fix packaging; few or non-repeatable errors do not justify training. If the untuned open model matches the comparator on retrieval-complete cases, prefer retrieval/prompting and skip training per the gate.
5. Before any GPU training, obtain separate rights-cleared, expert-reviewed training examples, with per-record licensing and reviewed decisive-evidence annotations; exclude untouched test and source-held-out data and deduplicate by structure and family. Check model license and redistribution restrictions. Freeze a base checkpoint, split, LoRA/QLoRA plan and reproducible settings, then compare tuned and untuned on the untouched test using the same prompts/seeds; budget, hardware and authorization are separate prerequisites. No such run is authorized here.
